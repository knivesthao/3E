# 3E demo video script (2–3 min, record on your phone)

Read this straight through while screen-recording. CapCut captions on top,
keyword zooms as marked. 9:16 if posting to TikTok/Reels, 16:9 for YouTube.

## Shot 1 — Problem (10s, ZOOM: "no books")

> "In Laos, fewer than one in seven kids owns even three books. Teachers
> need leveled reading material, and making it is slow. So I built 3E —
> it writes the books for them."

## Shot 2 — Write (20s, ZOOM: "3E Finish")

> "I start a comic, pick A1, write one scene about a girl and her cat —
> and hit 3E Finish. It writes the other five scenes for me, all at A1."

Do it live: Studio → New comic → A1 → one scene → Finish → scroll the pages.

## Shot 3 — Level check (15s, ZOOM: "out-of-level" + "Simplify")

> "Watch — it caught a word too hard for A1. One tap on Simplify, fixed.
> Every word in every book is checked like this."

Do it live: point at a flagged word, tap Simplify, show it pass.

## Shot 4 — Art + consistency (30s, ZOOM: "same cat, every page")

> "Muse Image draws every page, and the same cat shows up on every page —
> same look, panel after panel. Then I publish."

Do it live: generate art on 2 pages, flip through, tap Publish.

## Shot 5 — Cost (15s, ZOOM: "$0.06")

> "And here's the part teachers care about. The whole book — writing,
> checks, all six pictures — cost about six cents. Reading is free forever."

Do it live: open /admin/costs, point at the book row.

## Shot 6 — Read (20s, ZOOM: "tap any word")

> "On the phone: Pip reads the story out loud, highlighting each word. Tap
> any hard word for its meaning. Then the learner reads it back and gets
> scored — plus flashcards, the alphabet, and belt exams."

Do it live on the phone: open the book, buddy → Listen, tap a word, Read
one line, open Practice, finish on the alphabet card.

## Closer (5s)

> "3E — a book a day keeps illiteracy away. Link below."
